Ushbu hujjat Child Security (uz.childsecurity.app) ilovasining Google Play Store va xalqaro xavfsizlik standartlariga muvofiq rasmiy Maxfiylik Siyosatidir.

1. Maxfiylik Siyosati (O'zbek tilida)

1.1. Kirish va Maqsad

Child Security ("biz", "ilovamiz") oilaviy xavfsizlik va ota-ona nazorati tizimi hisoblanadi. Asosiy maqsadimiz — bolalarning shaxsiy xavfsizligini ta'minlash, yo'llarda va jamoat joylarida xavf-xatarlardan himoya qilish hamda ota-onaga bolasi haqida real vaqtda xotirjamlik bag'ishlashdir.

📍 1.2. Fondagi Doimiy Joylashuv (Background Location) Ma'lumotlari

Ilovamiz ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION va FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION ruxsatlaridan foydalanadi. Bu shuni anglatadiki, ilova yopiq bo'lsa yoki telefon ekrani o'chiq / bloklangan holatda ham bolaning xavfsizligini ta'minlash uchun GPS koordinatalari uzluksiz yangilanib turadi.

Ushbu ma'lumotlar nima uchun kerak?

  • Bola maktab, uy yoki to'garak kabi belgilangan xavfsiz hududlarga yetib kelganda yoki chiqib ketganda ota-onaga darhol avtomatik bildirishnoma yuborish (Geofencing).
  • Bola 🚨 SOS (Xavf tugmasi)ni bosganida ota-onaga bolaning aniq manzilini yetkazish.
  • Bolaning batareyasi tugab qolishidan oldin so'nggi ma'lum manzilni qayd etish.

1.3. Qanday ma'lumotlarni yig'amiz?

  • Geolokatsiya: Kenglik (latitude), uzunlik (longitude), aniqlik radiusi va tezlik.
  • Qurilma holati: Batareya foizi (%), quvvatlanish holati va ovoz rejimi (ovozli, tebranish, ovozsiz).
  • Hisob ma'lumotlari: Farzand ismi/taxallusi, 6 xonali ulanish kodi va Telegram foydalanuvchi identifikatori.

1.4. Ma'lumotlar xavfsizligi va Uchinchi shaxslarga bermaslik

Yig'ilgan barcha ma'lumotlar shifrlangan holda (HTTPS/SSL) uzatiladi. Biz sizning yoki bolangizning shaxsiy ma'lumotlarini hech qachon uchinchi shaxslarga sotmaymiz, ijaraga bermaymiz va reklama tarmoqlari bilan bo'lishmaymiz. Barcha ma'lumotlar faqatgina bola hisobiga ulangan rasmiy ota-onaga ko'rsatiladi.

1.5. Ma'lumotlarni o'chirish (Data Deletion)

Ota-ona xohlagan paytda saytdagi sozlamalar bo'limidan yoki Telegram bot orqali farzand profilini va unga tegishli barcha lokatsiya tarixini bir zumda butunlay o'chirib tashlashi mumkin.

2. Privacy Policy (English Version)

2.1. Overview & Purpose

Child Security (package: uz.childsecurity.app) is a dedicated parental control and personal safety application designed to help parents ensure the physical safety and wellbeing of their children.

📍 2.2. Prominent Disclosure: Background Location Tracking

Child Security collects and transmits location data in the background (including when the app is closed or not in use) using ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION permissions.

Background location data is used exclusively for:

  • Real-time automated geofence boundary alerts (notifying parents when their child safely enters or leaves designated safe zones such as home or school).
  • Emergency SOS panic alerts to transmit the child's exact coordinates to their designated parents immediately.
  • Speed monitoring and transit alerts to notify parents of vehicular travel.

2.3. No Sale or Third-Party Sharing of Personal Data

We do not sell, rent, or share personal location information or telemetry with any third-party advertisers or external data brokers. Telemetry is solely visible to the authenticated parental account linked via mutual consent pairing codes.

2.4. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

Users retain full control over their data. Parents can permanently wipe their family account, including all historical GPS logs and child profiles, at any time directly through the app settings or via the integrated Telegram bot interface.
